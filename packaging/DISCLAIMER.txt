AI-POWERED GOLD TRADING DECISION ASSISTANT
Important information — please read before installing


WHAT THIS SOFTWARE IS

This application is a decision-support and analytical tool for XAUUSD (Gold).
It studies the market across multiple timeframes, scores the technical
evidence, and presents a BUY, SELL or WAIT recommendation together with the
reasoning behind it.


WHAT THIS SOFTWARE IS NOT

It is not an automated trading system. It will not open, modify or close
trades, will not place pending orders, and will not manage your broker
account. It connects to MetaTrader 5 to READ price data only.

It is not financial advice, and it is not a licensed advisory service. No
recommendation it produces is a recommendation to buy or sell anything.


ABOUT THE SETUP SCORE

The score shown against every setup measures the weight of the technical
evidence the engine has found. It is NOT a probability of profit, and it does
not indicate how likely a trade is to win. A high score means several
independent forms of technical evidence agree — nothing more.
